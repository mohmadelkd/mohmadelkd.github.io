3D-printed clamp with heated inserts for a salvaged printer DC motor

1 / 3

The part

A salvaged HP Photosmart DC motor, a few M3 heated inserts, and a clamp that holds both together.

  • Clamps the motor body without squeezing the shaft
  • Two heated inserts on top let me bolt on attachments (a fan, a drill chuck…)
  • Modelled to print in place, no supports

Attachments

Anything that bolts onto the two inserts gets rotation from the motor. Next up: a high-pressure dust blower for cleaning the PC.

It's a component, not a build — it may end up inside a bigger project, or never.
