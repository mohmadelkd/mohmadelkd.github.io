The part

A simple printed shroud that makes the enclosure fan actually cool the amp board instead of stirring air in circles.

  • Directs the 60 mm fan straight over the heat sink
  • Snaps into the enclosure floor, no screws
  • Printed in PETG, 0.2 mm layers

It was built for the desktop amp, but it'd fit any flat-heatsink module.
