Split Mechanical Keyboard

May 18, 2025

Hand-wired columnar split with custom QMK firmware

1 / 6

The plan

A columnar-staggered split keyboard, hand-wired and running QMK. No PCB — a 3D-printed plate, a bag of switches, and a long evening with an iron.

  • Hand-wired 5 × 3 matrix per half plus a 3-key thumb cluster
  • Two Elite-C controllers with TRRS between halves
  • Rotary encoder on the left for volume

Wiring

Each column is a diode-or'd line; rows scan in software. The photo captions show the breadboard pass before I committed to solder.

Typing on it feels like flying. The stagger took one day to get used to.

Firmware and a JSON layout are in the files.
