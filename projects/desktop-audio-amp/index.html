Desktop Audio Amp

June 11, 2024

A small TPA3116 amp in a machined box, with a power filter

1 / 6

The project

A ~30 W per channel desktop amp built around a TPA3116D2 module, an aluminum enclosure, and a clean 19 V supply.

  • Added a power filter and a soft-start circuit
  • Replaced the module's cheap knobs with proper Alps potentiometers
  • Crowned it with a brushed front panel
Input -> vol pot -> TPA3116D2 -> speaker out
PSU   -> filter board -> module + soft-start

Listening

Flat frequency response, dead quiet on moderate gain. Thermal paste between the module and the case keeps it cool at a whole afternoon of lo-fi.

Full schematic and BOM are in the files.
