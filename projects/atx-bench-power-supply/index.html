The build

A dead 450 W ATX supply became the most-used thing on my bench. One weekend, a handful of parts, and a healthy respect for capacitors.

  • Kept the +12 V and +5 V rails, insulated the rest
  • Added a switchable output panel with banana jacks
  • Wired in a volt/amp meter with a 10 A shunt
Capacitors hold a deadly charge. I discharged everything twice before touching it.

Wiring

PSU +12V  ->  terminal strip  ->  meter  ->  banana jack (red)
PSU GND   ->  terminal strip  ->  meter shunt ->  banana jack (black)

What I'd change

A proper current-limit module beats relying on the PSU's own protection. Design notes and a parts list are in the files below.
