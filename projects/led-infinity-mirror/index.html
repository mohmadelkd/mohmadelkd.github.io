LED Infinity Mirror

November 2, 2025

Endless tunnel of light from a two-way mirror and a WS2812B ring

1 / 5

How it works

A two-way mirror facing a real mirror with LEDs between them. Light bounces back and forth, so the reflection looks infinite.

  • 24-pixel WS2812B ring
  • Acrylic disc with one-way mirror film
  • 3D-printed frame with a chamfered bezel

The firmware is short — it just walks the hue wheel:

void loop() {
  for (int i = 0; i < NUM_LEDS; i++) {
    leds[i] = CHSV(millis() / 8 + i * 16, 255, 255);
  }
  FastLED.show();
}

Power

A 5 V 3 A supply runs through a connector in the back. The ring draws about 1.4 A at full white, so I dialed brightness to 40%.

It looks amazing in a dark room — and doubles as a night light when I'm soldering.
