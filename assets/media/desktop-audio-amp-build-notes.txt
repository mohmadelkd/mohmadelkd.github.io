# Build notes (demo)

## Tools used
- Soldering station, flux, 63/37 solder
- Multimeter, bench supply
- Drill + step bits, deburring tool

## Steps
1. Lay out all the parts on the bench first.
2. Tack-solder the ground plane, then the signal side.
3. Inspect with a loupe — cold joints hide.
4. Power up on a current-limited supply before connecting real loads.

## Lessons
- A fuse on the output is non-negotiable.
- Label everything; future-you will thank present-you.
- Take photos before you close the box.
